---
title: Server Config
---

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `part_cap` | 24 | Most parts in one multiblock, not counting the [core](@modular_planters:core). Frames count. Allowed: 1 to 256. |
| `power_block_buffer` | 4,000,000 | RF each [power block](@modular_planters:power) holds |
| `me_interval` | 20 | Ticks between [ME block](@modular_planters:me) item moves and fertilizer top-ups. Allowed: 1 to 1200. |

ATE's numbers are never copied into this config. They are read from ATE.
