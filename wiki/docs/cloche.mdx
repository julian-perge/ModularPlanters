---
title: Built-in Cloche
---

The core applies a cloche to every planter block. It always works and needs no item. It uses ATE's configured cloche speed and yield bonuses.

ATE rounds a 1-item drop at 1.1 yield back to 1, so a cloche does nothing for Mystical Agriculture essence there. The built-in cloche keeps the fraction that rounding throws away and gives one extra item each time it adds up to 1. With the default numbers that is one extra item every tenth harvest.

See [core](@modular_planters:core) for the block that applies it.
