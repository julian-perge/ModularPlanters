---
title: Jade
---

With Jade installed, looking at a Modular Planter Core shows "Formed: N / cap parts", or "Not formed:" followed by the problem the core screen names. Looking at a Modular Planter Block shows its status and how many of its seeds are growing, as "growing/total seeds growing".

Both can be turned off in Jade's plugin settings. Jade is optional, and the mod runs without it.
