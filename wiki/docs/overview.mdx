---
title: Modular Planters
---

Modular Planters is a NeoForge addon for Agritech: Evolved (ATE) that replaces many Advanced Planters with a few multiblocks. A planter block with N growing seeds produces and draws what N Advanced Planters would, and one connection to the core runs the whole multiblock.

It targets Minecraft 26.1.2 and NeoForge 26.1.2.116 or newer, and it needs ATE 2.2.0 or newer. An optional ME block connects a multiblock to an AE2 network, with Applied Flux support. It needs AE2 and does not exist without it.

## Abbreviations

| Short | Full name |
|---|---|
| ATE | Agritech: Evolved |
| AE2 | Applied Energistics 2 |
| AE | AE2's own energy unit |
| RF, FE | NeoForge's energy unit, which mods call RF or FE |

## Blocks

| Block | Job |
|---|---|
| Core | Forms the multiblock, applies the built-in cloche, opens the core screen, and is the doorway for energy, fertilizer and outputs. It stores nothing itself. |
| Power | Stores energy. Several power blocks pool their storage. Energy goes in but can't be taken out. A broken power block keeps its energy in the dropped item. |
| Fertilizer | 27 slots shared by every planter block. It accepts only ATE fertilizers. |
| Export | 54 slots that hold outputs until something pulls them out. Only the multiblock can insert. Anything can extract. |
| Planter | Grows one seed type, up to 64 seeds. It has a seed slot, a soil slot and two upgrade slots. |
| Frame | Connects parts and does nothing else. |
| ME | Connects the multiblock to an AE2 network. It exists only when AE2 is installed. |

Fertilizer and export blocks drop their contents when broken.

The recipes are datapack JSON, so a modpack can replace them.
