The planter block grows one seed type, up to 64 seeds. It has a seed slot, a soil slot and two upgrade slots.

A planter block costs 8 [frames](@modular_planters:frame), so 64 Advanced Planters plus 8 blocks of iron and one Infused Farmland.

## Growing
- Growing seeds is the smaller of the seed count and the soil count. Seeds past that stay idle.
- One upgrade covers one seed. Upgrade 1 covers the first seeds in the bin and upgrade 2 does the same, so seeds fill both upgrades first.
- Seeds with the same upgrades and the same seed data grow as a group. Each seed levels on its own, as it would in its own Advanced Planter.
- Growth time, power and fertilizer follow ATE's formulas and are read from ATE's live config.
- When there is no room in the [export blocks](@modular_planters:export), planter blocks wait. Nothing is voided.
- Breaking a block drops everything it holds.

The [core](@modular_planters:core) applies a built-in cloche to every planter block. It always works and needs no item.
