The core forms the multiblock, applies the built-in cloche to every planter block, opens the core screen, and is the doorway for energy, fertilizer and outputs. It stores nothing itself.

## The core as a doorway
While the multiblock is formed, the core exposes two connections:

- Energy inserted into the core goes to the [power blocks](@modular_planters:power).
- Items inserted into the core go to the [fertilizer blocks](@modular_planters:fertilizer), and only fertilizers are accepted. Items extracted from the core come out of the [export blocks](@modular_planters:export).

So one Logistics Networks node on the core, or one cable or pipe, runs the whole multiblock. The power, fertilizer and export blocks also expose their own contents directly. While the multiblock is not formed, the core exposes nothing.

## Core screen
The core screen shows the formed state and part count, the reason when the multiblock is not formed, stored power and draw, fertilizer stored, how full the export blocks are, and the ME status. It lists every [planter block](@modular_planters:planter) with its seed, growing count, upgrade coverage, status and position.

Each row has an Outline toggle that shows or hides a glowing outline of that block, for you only, and an Open button that opens the planter block's screen remotely. The server checks that you are within 4 blocks of the core and that the planter block belongs to that core's formed multiblock.
