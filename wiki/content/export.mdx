The export block has 54 slots that hold outputs until something pulls them out. Only the multiblock can insert. Anything can extract. It drops its contents when broken.

Items extracted from the [core](@modular_planters:core) come out of the export blocks.

When there is no room in the export blocks, [planter blocks](@modular_planters:planter) wait. Nothing is voided. With an [ME block](@modular_planters:me), harvests go into ME first and whatever ME does not take goes into the export blocks.
