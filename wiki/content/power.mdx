The power block stores energy. Several power blocks pool their storage. Energy goes in but can't be taken out. A broken power block keeps its energy in the dropped item.

## Buffer
Each power block holds 4,000,000 RF by default. The `power_block_buffer` option in the server config sets it.

## How energy gets in
Energy inserted into the [core](@modular_planters:core) goes to the power blocks, and the power blocks also expose their own storage directly. With Applied Flux installed, a Flux Accessor placed against the core or a power block also charges it.

Power is drawn each tick for every seed that is still growing. If the power blocks can't cover a planter block's full draw, its seeds stop growing for that tick. Seeds that are already ready still harvest.

With Applied Flux installed, the [ME block](@modular_planters:me) fills the power blocks' free room from ME's FE every tick, so the power blocks stay full.
