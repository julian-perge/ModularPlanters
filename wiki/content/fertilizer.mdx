The fertilizer block has 27 slots shared by every planter block. It accepts only ATE fertilizers. It drops its contents when broken.

Items inserted into the [core](@modular_planters:core) go to the fertilizer blocks, and only fertilizers are accepted.

Fertilizer is taken from the first non-empty slot of the fertilizer blocks. Each tick, [planter blocks](@modular_planters:planter) claim it in position order, and each group claims one item for every one of its seeds or nothing. A group without a claim grows and harvests unfertilized that tick. A claim uses up fertilizer only when its group harvests.
