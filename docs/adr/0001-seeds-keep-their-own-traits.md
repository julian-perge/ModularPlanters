# Seeds keep their own traits

Other mods, such as Productive Farming, change a seed through ATE's planter events, and seeds whose data differs do not stack. A seed bin holds each seed as its own item with its own data and groups seeds with identical data, so every seed levels on its own and a Modular Planter Block matches the same number of Advanced Planters exactly.

Players moving from Advanced Planters can put in all their seeds without sorting them, and the seed slot's tooltip lists how many seeds have each trait combination.
